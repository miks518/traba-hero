Trabahero — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Trabahero is a browser extension that helps Filipino job seekers assess job postings for scam signals, verify the named employer, and match a resume against scanned postings. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

1. Data we process

  • Job posting content. When you choose to scan a posting, the part of the page you select is captured as an image (or text) and sent to the Trabahero backend for analysis.
  • Resume content. If you upload a resume for matching, its contents are sent to the backend to extract skills and experience. A resume may contain personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and work history.
  • Local settings. Your theme, text size, floating-button preference, and scan history are stored in your browser using chrome.storage.local. These stay on your device.

2. How we use it

Submitted content is used solely to perform the analysis you requested — assessing a posting, verifying an employer, or matching a resume. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to that request.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not build user accounts, and we do not track your browsing history.

4. Retention

Screenshots and resume text are processed in memory to complete the request. They are not persisted by the backend after the response is returned. Scan history is stored only in your browser and can be cleared at any time from the extension.

5. Third-party services

To analyse content, the Trabahero backend sends it to an AI provider (OpenRouter) and, for employer verification, to a web-search provider (Tavily). These providers process the content only to return a result for your request. No personal account information is sent to them.

6. Permissions

  • activeTab / tabs — to capture the posting you select on the active tab.
  • host permissions — job postings appear on arbitrary websites; the extension must read the specific region you pick.
  • storage — to keep your settings and scan history on your device.
  • sidePanel — to show results beside the posting.

7. Children

Trabahero is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.
